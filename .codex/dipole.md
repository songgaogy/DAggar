# DIPOLE Framework Implementation Request

## Overview
Please implement the **DIPOLE (Dichotomous Diffusion Policy Optimization)** reinforcement learning framework in PyTorch. 
- Reference Paper: [arXiv:2601.00898](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.00898)
- Official Codebase: `https://github.com/Whiterrrrr/dipole-rl/tree/67c8e035fc40afc51d62488c9a039030e8498cfd/ogbench`

DIPOLE decomposes the optimal policy into a pair of stably learned dichotomous diffusion policies:
1. **Reward Maximization Policy ($\pi^+$)**
2. **Reward Minimization Policy ($\pi^-$)**

During inference, optimized actions are generated by linearly combining the score outputs of these two dichotomous policies.

## Mathematical Formulation

### 1. Training Objective
The training relies on a weighted diffusion loss for both policies. Let $\epsilon_\theta^+$ and $\epsilon_\theta^-$ be the noise prediction networks. The objectives are:

$$\mathcal{L}^+(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{s, a, t, \epsilon} \left[ w^+(s, a) \| \epsilon - \epsilon_\theta^+(a_t | s, t) \|^2 \right]$$

$$\mathcal{L}^-(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{s, a, t, \epsilon} \left[ w^-(s, a) \| \epsilon - \epsilon_\theta^-(a_t | s, t) \|^2 \right]$$

Where $w^+$ and $w^-$ are advantage-based weighting functions for maximization and minimization, respectively.

### 2. Inference (Action Generation)
During the reverse diffusion process, the predicted noise is a linear combination of the two dichotomous policies governed by a guidance scale $\omega$:

$$\tilde{\epsilon}_\theta(a_t | s) = (1 + \omega) \epsilon_\theta^+(a_t | s) - \omega \epsilon_\theta^-(a_t | s)$$
