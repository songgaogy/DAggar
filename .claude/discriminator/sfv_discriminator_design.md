# Dichotomous Density-Ratio Discriminator (D³-Disc)

> **A DIPOLE-inspired, density-ratio-based, multi-task failure detector built on top of the pretrained Flow-Multi policy encoder.**
>
> **Design goals**:
> 1. Strict *superset* of current LPB — exact recovery at `ω=0`.
> 2. **Make first-class use of fail_rollout data** (current LPB ignores it), while explicitly handling the "dirty-fail" problem.
> 3. Discriminator values carry a rigorous IRL-optimal-reward meaning: `G(s,a) = log p_+ − log p_-`, i.e. the conditional log-odds of success vs failure.
> 4. Keep LPB's non-parametric KDE / KNN + conformal calibration benefits.
>
> **Revision history**:
> - v1 (SFV-Disc, deprecated): used only success data, rollout-discounted log-density. Judged as "KNN wrapped in RL sugar".
> - v2 (this doc, D³-Disc): adds dichotomous decomposition using fail data + self-consistent filtering.
>
> **Scope locked (per user decisions D1–D12)**: K=0 step-wise only (no dynamics rollout in phase 1); shared `p_+` and `p_-` across tasks; flow_multi policy encoder as the feature backbone; F3 static self-consistent filter; ω default 0.5, sweep {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1}.

## 1. Notation & Problem Setup

### 1.1 MDP

$\mathcal{M} = (\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, P, \gamma, \rho_0)$, no explicit reward — reward is constructed from data.

### 1.2 Two data distributions

- **Success distribution** $\rho_+(s,a)$: state-action occupancy induced by successful rollouts.
- **Fail distribution** $\rho_-(s,a)$: raw occupancy of failed rollouts. Note: *raw* $\rho_-$ is **dirty** — most prefix frames look identical to $\rho_+$; only the suffix after the "point of no return" is genuinely off-support. Cleaning is handled in §6.

Empirical datasets:
$$
\mathcal{D}_+ = \{(s_i, a_i)\}_{i=1}^{N_+},\qquad \mathcal{D}_-^{\text{raw}} = \{(s_j, a_j)\}_{j=1}^{N_-}
$$

### 1.3 Feature encoder — Flow-Multi policy encoder (D4)

We reuse the **pretrained Flow-Multi policy encoder** (located at `robosuite/policy/flow_multi/`) as the shared feature map. The encoder path is:

$$
\varphi(o_t, s_t^{\text{proprio}}) \;=\; \mathrm{AGG}\!\left(\mathrm{Fusion}\!\left(\mathrm{ImgEnc}(o_t),\; \mathrm{ProprioTok}(s_t^{\text{proprio}}),\; \mathrm{LangEnc}(\ell_{\text{task}})\right)\right) \;\in \mathbb{R}^{256}
$$

- `ImgEnc`: `SharedResNetSpatialSoftmaxEncoder` (ResNet-18 + SpatialSoftmax + multi-level projections).
- `Fusion`: language-conditioned Transformer with FiLM modulation.
- `AGG`: `condition_aggregator` outputting a single 256-D token per frame (`model_cfg.aggregator.output_dim`).

**Cache convention (already generated by a previous pipeline)**:
- Path: `data/.lpb_score_cache/<task_name>/<sha1>.npz`
- Contents: `latents: (T, 256) float32`, `actions: (T, 7) float32`, `task_name`, `file_path`, `demo_key`
- Hash key: `sha1(task|resolved_file_path|demo_key|image_size|camera_names|file_mtime|checkpoint_path)`
- 1.1 GB on disk, ~650 demos/task across 6 tasks, lit-up via `LPBScoreCache` reader.

**Feature definition used by D³-Disc**:

$$
\varphi_t \;\triangleq\; \left[\; z_t \;\oplus\; \mathrm{Proj}_a(a_t) \;\right] \;\in\; \mathbb{R}^{256 + d_a}
$$

where $z_t \in \mathbb{R}^{256}$ comes from the cache (or on-the-fly encoding for cache miss), and $\mathrm{Proj}_a$ is either identity (D5 option A, 1-NN default) or a learned linear projection sharing dimensionality with $z$ (phase-2 option).

**Why flow_multi encoder rather than LPB's own ResNet?** (i) The flow encoder is already trained on the exact task distribution of interest via imitation, so its latent geometry is aligned with task semantics. (ii) Cache already exists. (iii) Ensures discriminator and policy live in the same representation space, enabling downstream couplings.

### 1.4 Task

Per test trajectory $\{(o_t, s_t^{\text{proprio}}, a_t)\}_{t=0}^{T-1}$ from an unseen task instance, emit $\lambda_t \in \mathbb{R}$ and a binary prediction $\hat y_t$, targeting false-alarm rate $\le \delta$ under conformal calibration.

### 1.5 Multi-task sharing (D8)

We build **one shared** $\hat p_+$ and **one shared** $\hat p_-$ across all tasks (pooled banks). The hope is failure modes transfer across tasks (e.g., "gripper slip" or "object drop" looks similar in flow-multi latent space across `PickPlaceBread`/`PickPlaceMilk`). Per-task calibration threshold $\tau$ is still allowed — see §8.3.

## 2. LPB revisited through an RL lens

### 2.1 KNN = KDE log-density

With a Gaussian kernel:
$$
\hat p_+(\varphi) \;=\; \frac{1}{N_+}\sum_{i=1}^{N_+} \frac{1}{(2\pi\sigma_+^2)^{d/2}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{\|\varphi - \varphi_i\|^2}{2\sigma_+^2}\right)
$$
When the nearest neighbor dominates (typical in high-dimensional spaces):
$$
\log \hat p_+(\varphi) \;\approx\; -\frac{d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi, \mathcal{D}_+)}{2\sigma_+^2} + C
$$

### 2.2 LPB step-score

$$
\text{step}_t^{\text{LPB}} \;=\; d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi_t, \mathcal{D}_+) \;\approx\; -2\sigma_+^2 \log \hat p_+(s_t, a_t) + C'
$$

i.e. **negative log success-density**, up to positive scale and constant. Percentile threshold is scale-invariant ⇒ effectively `step ∝ −log p_+`.

## 3. Why one-sided KNN is not enough

1. **Waste of fail data**: even after cleaning, fail trajectories contain many frames that are genuinely off the success manifold. LPB ignores them entirely.
2. **Failure to distinguish "rare good" from "typical bad"**: a novel yet on-task $(s,a)$ and a typical post-failure $(s,a)$ both yield large $d_{\text{knn}}^2$ — but only the latter is a true failure.
3. **Suboptimal from IRL perspective**: GAIL/AIRL show the optimal reward is $\log(\rho_+/\rho_-)$; using only $\log \rho_+$ is a strict under-use of available information.

The fix is to bring $\hat p_-$ into the picture. But naively fitting $\hat p_-$ on the raw fail set would poison the estimator with expert-like prefix frames. §6 addresses this.

## 4. The DIPOLE ingredient we borrow

DIPOLE (Liang et al. ICLR 2026) treats the numerically-unstable exponential weight in KL-regularized RL, $\pi^\star \propto \mu \exp(\beta G)$, via an algebraic identity that factors the optimal policy into two bounded, sigmoid-weighted components:
$$
\pi^\star \propto \mu \cdot \sigma(\beta G)^{1+\omega} \cdot (1-\sigma(\beta G))^{-\omega} \;=\; \frac{[\pi^+]^{1+\omega}}{[\pi^-]^{\omega}}
$$
with log-form (DIPOLE Eq. 10):
$$
\log \pi^\star(a|s) \;=\; (1+\omega)\log \pi^+(a|s) \;-\; \omega \log \pi^-(a|s) \;+\; \text{const} \tag{$\star$}
$$

Three properties we reuse:
- **Bounded**: $\sigma(\cdot), 1-\sigma(\cdot) \in (0,1)$ — numerically stable.
- **ω=0 degeneration**: $\log \pi^\star \to \log \pi^+$, automatically collapses to the positive-only baseline.
- **ω smoothly interpolates**: between "pure positive support" (ω=0) and "sharp positive-vs-negative contrast" (ω→∞).

We do **not** train a policy. We just reuse the log-linear combination structure of ($\star$) as the discriminator formula.

## 5. D³-Disc core construction

### 5.1 Two density estimators

- $\hat p_+(\varphi)$: 1-NN KDE on pooled success features $\mathcal{D}_+$ from all tasks (exactly the current LPB bank, extended to multi-task).
- $\hat p_-(\varphi)$: **weighted** 1-NN KDE on fail features $\mathcal{D}_-$ with soft weights $w_t^- \in (0,1)$ from §6.

### 5.2 Step-wise score (main formula)

Adapting DIPOLE Eq. ($\star$) with $\mu \equiv$ uniform (no reference policy in a discriminator):
$$
\boxed{
\quad \lambda_t^{\mathrm{D}^3} \;\triangleq\; -(1+\omega)\log \hat p_+(\varphi_t) \;+\; \omega \log \hat p_-(\varphi_t) \quad
}
\tag{$\dagger$}
$$
In 1-NN KDE form (taking $\sigma_+ = \sigma_- = 1/\sqrt{2}$, since ranking is scale-invariant under percentile calibration):
$$
\lambda_t^{\mathrm{D}^3} \;=\; (1+\omega)\, d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi_t, \mathcal{D}_+) \;-\; \omega\, d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi_t, \widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_-) \;+\; \text{const}
$$
where $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_-$ is the cleaned (weighted) fail bank from §6.

### 5.3 Decomposition: baseline + correction

$$
\lambda_t^{\mathrm{D}^3} \;=\; \underbrace{-\log \hat p_+(\varphi_t)}_{\text{LPB baseline}} \;+\; \omega \cdot \underbrace{\left[\log \hat p_-(\varphi_t) - \log \hat p_+(\varphi_t)\right]}_{=:\;-G(\varphi_t)}
$$

$G(\varphi) := \log \hat p_+ - \log \hat p_-$ is the **IRL-optimal reward** (see §7.1).

### 5.4 1-NN vs k-NN (D5)

Default: 1-NN min squared distance (matches current LPB). The `k` parameter is exposed on the bash entry for ablation:
$$
d_{\text{knn},k}^2(\varphi, \mathcal{D}) \;=\; \frac{1}{k}\sum_{j \in \mathrm{kNN}_k(\varphi, \mathcal{D})} \|\varphi - \varphi_j\|^2
$$
Larger `k` is more robust but blurs sharp manifold boundaries.

### 5.5 Discriminator rule

$$
\hat y_t \;=\; \mathbb{1}[\lambda_t^{\mathrm{D}^3} \ge \tau],\qquad \tau = \mathrm{Quantile}_{1-\delta}\!\big(\{\lambda^{\text{calib}}_j\}_j\big)
$$
Calibration is over held-out success frames; conformal guarantee holds on the full D³ score (D6 choice A) — see §8.

## 6. Handling dirty fail data — F3

> **F3 is the chosen filter (D3). F1/F2 are dropped.** This section gives the full definition and a new **consistency theorem** added per D12.

### 6.1 Self-consistent soft-reweighting

Use **only** $\hat p_+$ to assign each fail frame a soft weight (the "filter"):
$$
\boxed{\; w_t^- \;=\; \sigma\!\big(\beta \cdot [d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi_t, \mathcal{D}_+) \;-\; \kappa]\big) \;\in (0,1) \;}
$$
- $\kappa$: the **median self-KNN distance** on the success bank (exclude-self). Data-driven; no manual tuning.
- $\beta$: inverse temperature. Default $\beta = 1 / \mathrm{MAD}(d_{\text{knn}}^2 \mid \mathcal{D}_+)$. Exposed as a bash CLI flag for ablation.

**Semantics**:
- Fail frame far from success support ($d_{\text{knn}}^2 \gg \kappa$) ⇒ $w_t^- \to 1$, fully included in $\hat p_-$.
- Fail frame inside success support ($d_{\text{knn}}^2 \ll \kappa$) ⇒ $w_t^- \to 0$, softly rejected.

### 6.2 Weighted 1-NN KDE for $\hat p_-$

Replace the vanilla 1-NN with a weight-aware soft-min evaluated only on fail frames:
$$
\log \hat p_-(\varphi) \;\approx\; \log\!\left(\frac{1}{W}\sum_{j \in \mathcal{D}_-} w_j^- \exp(-\|\varphi - \varphi_j\|^2 / (2\sigma_-^2))\right)
$$
with $W = \sum_j w_j^-$. Computed chunk-wise like the existing `knn_min_sqdist` in `knn_discriminator.py`.

For the 1-NN approximation (D5 default):
$$
d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi, \widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_-) \;\triangleq\; \min_{j \in \mathcal{D}_-} \left[\|\varphi - \varphi_j\|^2 \;-\; 2\sigma_-^2 \log w_j^-\right]
$$
which is the MAP estimator of $-2\sigma_-^2 \log \hat p_-$ with soft weights folded into an additive penalty (low-weight fail points are pushed "further away" in effective distance).

### 6.3 Four virtues of F3

1. **Self-consistent**: $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_-$ is computed from $\hat p_+$ alone, no extra model, no GT mask.
2. **DIPOLE-coherent**: $w_t^- = \sigma(\beta \cdot \text{stuff})$ matches DIPOLE's own sigmoid-weighted structure, consistent from filter to score.
3. **Safe degeneration**: if fail data happens to lie entirely on the success manifold (success and fail overlap completely), all $w_j^- \to 0$, $\hat p_-$ collapses, and $\lambda^{\mathrm{D}^3} \to \lambda^{\text{LPB}}$ — the system *automatically discovers that fail data is uninformative* and falls back to baseline.
4. **Differentiable**: the pipeline is smooth in $\beta$, enabling future meta-learning of the filter temperature.

### 6.4 Consistency of F3 (theorem, added per D12)

**Setting**. Let $\mathcal{D}_+ = \{\varphi_i\}_{i=1}^{N_+}$ be i.i.d. samples from a true success distribution with density $p_+$. Let $\mathcal{S}_+ := \operatorname{supp}(p_+)$. Define the ground-truth off-support indicator $y^\star(\varphi) := \mathbb{1}[\varphi \notin \mathcal{S}_+]$.

**Assumptions**.
- **(A1) KDE consistency**: $\sup_\varphi |\hat p_+(\varphi; N_+) - p_+(\varphi)| \xrightarrow{P} 0$ as $N_+ \to \infty$. (Parzen-Rosenblatt standard conditions: bandwidth $\sigma_+(N_+) \to 0$ with $N_+ \sigma_+(N_+)^d \to \infty$.)
- **(A2) Feature separability**: the feature map $\varphi$ is sufficiently rich that $\rho_-$ places non-zero mass on $\mathcal{S}_+^c$ (otherwise fail is indistinguishable from success in feature space).

**Schedule**. Take $\beta_{N_+} \to \infty$ and $\kappa_{N_+} \to 0^+$ as $N_+ \to \infty$. The proposed default $\kappa = $ success self-KNN median satisfies $\kappa_{N_+} \to 0$ automatically as the bank densifies.

**Claim**. Under (A1), (A2), and the schedule above,
$$
w^-_t \;=\; \sigma\!\big(\beta_{N_+}[d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi_t, \mathcal{D}_+) - \kappa_{N_+}]\big) \;\xrightarrow{N_+ \to \infty}\; y^\star(\varphi_t) \;\in \{0, 1\}.
$$

**Sketch of proof** (two-case argument).
- If $\varphi_t \in \mathcal{S}_+$: by support-density, $\exists$ infinitely many $\varphi_i \in \mathcal{D}_+$ arbitrarily close to $\varphi_t$, so $d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi_t, \mathcal{D}_+) \to 0 \le \kappa_{N_+}$. Then $\sigma(\beta_{N_+} \cdot \text{non-positive}) \to 0$.
- If $\varphi_t \notin \mathcal{S}_+$: distance to support has a positive lower bound $\delta_0 > 0$, so $d_{\text{knn}}^2 \ge \delta_0^2$ eventually exceeds $\kappa_{N_+} \to 0$. Then $\sigma(\beta_{N_+} \cdot \text{positive}) \to 1$.

**Corollary**. The fixed point of static F3 in the data limit is **exactly the off-support set of $\rho_+$**. This is precisely the correct target for the denominator of the IRL-optimal reward $G = \log(p_+/p_-)$: prefix frames that coincide with success occupancy are correctly rejected from $\hat p_-$, and suffix/bifurcation frames that genuinely deviate are correctly included.

**Practical implication**. We do not need an iterative EM loop (D11 chooses static); the one-shot computation $w^- = \sigma(\beta [d_{\text{knn}}^2(\varphi, \mathcal{D}_+) - \kappa])$ is statistically consistent with the ideal filter given enough success data.

### 6.5 Caveat: off-support vs. "real failure"

"Off-support of $\rho_+$ in state-action space" is not identical to a human-annotated "real failure moment", but it is the **correct** construction for use inside $G = \log(p_+/p_-)$. Specifically:
- Prefix of a fail trajectory where success policy would also produce the same $(s,a)$ ⇒ on-support ⇒ filtered out ⇒ *correctly*, because these frames should not be in $\hat p_-$ (they would make $\hat p_-$ resemble $\hat p_+$ and cancel $G$).
- Bifurcation moment: state still normal but action is wrong. Because $\varphi$ is a **joint state-action feature**, $(s_t, a_t)$ is off the $\rho_+$ support even if $s_t$ alone is on the success state support. F3 captures this.
- Late suffix after failure committed: both $s$ and $a$ drift off manifold ⇒ strongly off-support ⇒ included with weight near 1.

## 7. RL/IL theoretical grounding

### 7.1 $G = \log p_+ - \log p_-$ is the IRL-optimal reward

By Bayes decomposition:
$$
\log \frac{p_+(s,a)}{p_-(s,a)} \;=\; \log \frac{\Pr(\text{success} \mid s,a)}{\Pr(\text{fail} \mid s,a)} \;+\; \log \frac{\Pr(\text{fail})}{\Pr(\text{success})}
$$
The first term is the **conditional log-odds of success**. AIRL (Fu et al. 2018) proves that the optimal reward function in a GAN-IRL objective is exactly $r^\star = \log D^\star - \log(1-D^\star)$, which equals $\log(\rho_+/\rho_-)$. Hence D³-Disc's reward term $r^{\mathrm{D}^3}$ is aligned with the AIRL-optimal reward in the $\omega \to \infty$ limit.

### 7.2 DIPOLE ↔ D³-Disc exact mapping

| DIPOLE (policy) | D³-Disc (discriminator) |
|---|---|
| Reference policy $\mu(a\|s)$ | Uniform base measure (dropped) |
| Advantage $G(s,a)$ | $\log(\hat p_+ / \hat p_-)$ — IRL-optimal reward |
| $\pi^+ \propto \mu \cdot \sigma(\beta G)$ | $\hat p_+$: success KDE (LPB bank) |
| $\pi^- \propto \mu \cdot (1-\sigma(\beta G))$ | $\hat p_-$: cleaned fail KDE |
| Eq. 10 log-combination | Eq. ($\dagger$) |
| $\omega$ = greediness | $\omega$ = fail-repulsion strength |

The key difference: DIPOLE *learns* $\pi^+, \pi^-$ with diffusion loss; we use non-parametric KDE for $\hat p_+, \hat p_-$ directly — we borrow the **optimal-distribution decomposition structure** of DIPOLE Eq. 7/8/10 without borrowing its training machinery.

### 7.3 Bidirectional MOPO interpretation

$$
r^{\mathrm{D}^3} \;=\; \underbrace{\log \hat p_+}_{\text{+support reward}} \;+\; \omega \cdot \underbrace{\log(\hat p_+ / \hat p_-)}_{\text{−manifold penalty}}
$$

The first term rewards remaining on the success manifold (standard MOPO uncertainty penalty flipped to a reward). The second term penalizes entering the fail manifold. Together they form a **bidirectional MOPO-style shaping**.

### 7.4 Connections to VICE / f-IRL

VICE (Fu et al. 2018) and f-IRL (Ni et al. 2021) use $\log(p_+/p_-)$ as an inverse RL reward signal. D³-Disc reproduces the same signal in an offline, non-parametric, discriminator-only setting.

## 8. Conformal calibration

### 8.1 Split conformal guarantee

With calibration set $\{\lambda^{\text{calib}}_j\}_{j=1}^M$ drawn from held-out success trajectories and threshold
$$
\tau \;=\; \lceil (1-\delta)(M+1) \rceil\text{-th smallest of }\{\lambda^{\text{calib}}_j\}
$$
we have, under exchangeability between calibration and test success frames,
$$
\Pr[\lambda^{\text{test}} \ge \tau \mid \text{test frame is success}] \;\le\; \delta.
$$
This guarantee is **independent of the form of $\lambda$** — substituting the D³ score does not break it (D6 choice A).

### 8.2 Exchangeability maintenance

- Both calibration and test frames must come from success distribution.
- $\hat p_-$ is built from **all** fail data (not split) and used identically across calibration and test frames ⇒ no asymmetry ⇒ exchangeability preserved.

### 8.3 Per-task vs shared threshold (under D8 shared banks)

Even though $\hat p_+$ and $\hat p_-$ are pooled across tasks, the calibration quantile $\tau$ can still be computed per task (recommended), since different tasks may have different score distributions on success trajectories. Details:
- Banks: shared across all 5–6 tasks.
- Calibration set: partitioned by task; compute a separate $\tau^{(\text{task})}$ from each task's held-out success pool.
- Alternative (ablation): single shared $\tau$ over all pooled calibration frames.

## 9. Degeneration & superset structure

D³-Disc is a **strict generalization** of current LPB along the $\omega$ axis.

| $\omega$ | Filter | Recovered method |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | — | **current LPB** (pooled multi-task variant) ✅ |
| > 0, F3 with $\beta \to 0$ | — | Near-LPB (fail data effectively neutralized) |
| > 0, F3 default | F3 | **D³-Disc** (main method) ✨ |
| $\omega \to \infty$ | F3 | Pure AIRL density-ratio discriminator |

**Ablation sweep** (D1 decision): `ω ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1}`.

## 11. Known limitations

### 11.1 Fail data scarcity or single-mode

If $\mathcal{D}_-$ is small or covers only a narrow set of failure modes, $\hat p_-$ is noisy on unseen failure types. **Mitigation**: F3's automatic fallback (see §6.3 virtue 3); keep $\omega$ moderate ($\le 1$); optionally add uniform prior smoothing on $\hat p_-$.

### 11.2 PandaLift has no fail_rollout

`PandaLift` has zero fail trajectories in the dataset. Under shared multi-task banks (D8), this task still benefits from $\hat p_-$ built from other tasks — but the utility depends on whether failure modes transfer. **Planned experiment**: report D³-Disc performance with/without `PandaLift` held-out; compare against LPB baseline on `PandaLift` (where the two should roughly match since $\omega > 0$ only adds cross-task signal).

### 11.3 F3 hyperparameters $\beta, \kappa$

$\kappa$ = success self-KNN median is robust. $\beta$ = $1/\mathrm{MAD}$ is a reasonable default, but effective sample size of $\hat p_-$ depends on it. **Ablation**: sweep $\beta \in \{0.5, 1, 2, 4\} \times \text{MAD}^{-1}$.

### 11.4 Multi-task bank may dilute task-specific signals

Pooling $\hat p_+$ across tasks can make a frame look "in-support" just because some other task lives nearby in latent space. **Mitigation**: per-task calibration threshold (§8.3); ablation with per-task $\hat p_+$.

### 11.5 Conformal exchangeability under task shift

Calibration and test must both be success frames from the same task distribution. Cross-task generalization is not automatically conformal-valid. Per-task $\tau$ addresses this.

### 11.6 Off-support ≠ human-annotated failure

Discussed in §6.5. For the density-ratio reward perspective this is correct; for a human-interpretable "when did it fail" question the answer may need re-aggregation.

### 11.7 K-step rollout deferred

Per D2, phase 1 is `K=0` (step-wise only). The K-step rollout extension (previously Section 7 of v1) is deferred to phase 2. Placeholder: once D³-Disc-step is validated, extend by computing $\lambda_t$ along a K-step latent rollout and taking discounted sum with $\gamma \in (0,1)$.

## Appendix A — DIPOLE-to-D³-Disc full derivation

### A.1 DIPOLE starting point

DIPOLE Eq. 5 (greedified KL-regularized objective):
$$
\max_\pi \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d^\pi}\!\left[\mathbb{E}_{a \sim \pi}[G(s,a)] - \tfrac{1}{\omega\beta} D_{\text{KL}}\!\left(\pi(\cdot|s)\,\Big\|\,\mu(\cdot|s) \cdot \frac{\sigma(\beta G)}{Z(s)}\right)\right]
$$
Closed form (DIPOLE Eq. 6):
$$
\pi^\star(a|s) \;\propto\; \mu(a|s) \cdot \sigma(\beta G(s,a)) \cdot \exp(\omega \beta G(s,a))
$$

### A.2 Sigmoid identity

Using $\sigma(x) \cdot \exp(\omega x) = \sigma(x) \cdot (\sigma(x)/(1-\sigma(x)))^\omega = \sigma(x)^{1+\omega}/(1-\sigma(x))^\omega$:
$$
\pi^\star \;\propto\; \mu \cdot \frac{\sigma(\beta G)^{1+\omega}}{(1-\sigma(\beta G))^\omega} \;=\; \frac{[\mu \sigma(\beta G)]^{1+\omega}}{[\mu (1-\sigma(\beta G))]^{\omega}} \cdot \mu^{-(1 + \omega) + \omega + 1}
$$
Simplifying the $\mu^\ast$ exponent (= 0) gives DIPOLE Eq. 7:
$$
\pi^\star \;\propto\; \frac{[\pi^+]^{1+\omega}}{[\pi^-]^\omega}
$$
with $\pi^+ \propto \mu \sigma(\beta G)$, $\pi^- \propto \mu(1 - \sigma(\beta G))$.

### A.3 Log-domain (DIPOLE Eq. 10)

$$
\log \pi^\star \;=\; (1+\omega)\log \pi^+ \;-\; \omega \log \pi^- \;+\; \mathrm{const}
$$

### A.4 Discriminator reparametrization

Set $\mu \equiv$ uniform (unbiased base measure), and choose $G := \log \hat p_+ - \log \hat p_-$. Then with $\beta = 1$:
$$
\sigma(G) \;=\; \frac{\hat p_+}{\hat p_+ + \hat p_-},\qquad 1-\sigma(G) \;=\; \frac{\hat p_-}{\hat p_+ + \hat p_-}
$$
i.e. $\pi^+(\varphi)$ and $\pi^-(\varphi)$ are exactly the Bayes posteriors of "success" and "fail" given $\varphi$. Substituting into the log-combination:
$$
\log \pi^\star \;=\; (1+\omega)\log \pi^+ \;-\; \omega \log \pi^- \;=\; (1+\omega)\log \hat p_+ \;-\; \omega \log \hat p_- \;-\; \log(\hat p_+ + \hat p_-) + C
$$
For a discriminator that only uses **ranking**, the term $\log(\hat p_+ + \hat p_-)$ is a shared slowly-varying denominator (dominated by the larger term in high-D KDE) and does not change failure ordering. The ranking-relevant signal is Eq. ($\dagger$):
$$
\lambda^{\mathrm{D}^3} \;=\; -(1+\omega)\log \hat p_+ \;+\; \omega \log \hat p_-.
$$

### A.5 Limits

- $\omega = 0$: $\lambda^{\mathrm{D}^3} = -\log \hat p_+$, exactly the LPB score.
- $\omega \to \infty$: ranking by $\lambda^{\mathrm{D}^3}/\omega \to \log \hat p_- - \log \hat p_+ = -G$, the pure AIRL density-ratio discriminator.
